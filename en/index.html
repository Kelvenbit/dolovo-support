Common questions

Why can’t I download some videos?

A video playing on a webpage does not mean it supports downloading. Availability depends on the website, format and access permissions. Live streams and DRM-protected videos are not supported. Only save content you are authorized to download where the source service permits it.

Can I watch without an internet connection?

Compatible videos can be played offline once they are fully saved on your device. An unfinished download or a video you have only streamed in the browser is not necessarily available offline.

How do I restore my purchases?

Make sure your device uses the Apple Account you used for the purchase. Open Dolovo’s purchase screen and select Restore Purchases. If your access is still missing, contact us using the email above.

Are Premium and Unlimited subscriptions?

Both are one-time purchases with no auto-renewing subscription. Free allows 3 saves per day, Premium allows 10 and Unlimited has no daily download count limit. Paid versions do not add support for more websites or formats.
